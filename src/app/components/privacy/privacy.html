Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Blue Plaques Explorer is a project for exploring London's commemorative plaques. This page explains, in plain words, what information the app keeps about you and why.

What we keep when you create an account

  • Username – shown next to your visits and photos.
  • Email address – used to check it's yours with a one-time code and to reset your password. It is never shown to other users.
  • Password – stored only as a secure hash (bcrypt), never as the password itself.
  • The date you agreed to these terms.

What you choose to add

  • Visits – your ratings, note and the date. Other users can see them with your username.
  • Photos – shown publicly with your username. Every photo is re-saved by our server, which removes hidden data such as the GPS location where it was taken.
  • Favourites – visible only to you.
  • Problem reports – visible only to administrators.

Your location

The map only asks for your location when you use it. Your position is used to find nearby plaques and, if you ask for directions, it is sent to Stadia Maps to work out the walking route. We do not save your location.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare Turnstile – checks that sign-ups come from a person, not a bot.
  • Stadia Maps – map images and walking directions.
  • Google Maps – only if you press "Open in Google Maps"; this opens Google's own site.
  • Google – only if you choose "Continue with Google". Google confirms your email address to us, and we save it with your Google account ID so you can sign in again.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Google's and Cloudflare's code is only loaded on the Log in and Sign up pages, so they can't see the rest of your visit.

How you stay logged in

When you log in, your browser stores a login token for 30 minutes. Logging out, changing your password or deleting your account ends it straight away.

Your choices

  • See your details, visits and favourites on My profile.
  • Change your password or delete your account in Account settings. Deleting your account also deletes your visits and photos.

See also the Terms of use.
